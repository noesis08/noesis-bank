Founded on Knowledge

NOESIS Bank was established in 1987 by a group of visionary economists and bankers who believed that financial institutions should be built on the Greek principle of noesis — deep understanding and intellectual insight.

What began as a single branch in New York City has grown into a global institution serving over 2.3 million customers across 150+ locations worldwide. Yet our founding philosophy remains unchanged: every financial decision should be guided by wisdom, not just numbers.

"We don't just manage money. We cultivate prosperity through knowledge."

Today, NOESIS stands at the intersection of traditional banking values and cutting-edge fintech innovation. We are proud to be recognized as one of the most trusted banks in North America, with an A+ rating from all major credit agencies.

Our Journey

Key milestones that shaped NOESIS into what it is today.

1987

The Beginning

NOESIS Bank opens its first branch on Wall Street, New York, with a team of 12 dedicated professionals.

1995

National Expansion

Expanded to 25 branches across the United States, introducing our first digital banking terminal.

2004

Global Reach

Opened international offices in London, Singapore, and Dubai, serving corporate clients worldwide.

2015

Digital Revolution

Launched NOESIS Mobile, our award-winning banking app with biometric security and AI-powered insights.

2024

2 Million Customers

Celebrated serving over 2 million customers with $47 billion in assets under management.
